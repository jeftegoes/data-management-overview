# 1. Ethical Principles Summary

- This summary outlines the ethical principles guiding responsible data management.
- These principles are core to the DAMA-DMBOK2, emphasizing fairness, transparency, and accountability in data practices.

| Principle          | Definition                                                                           | Practical Example                                                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Fairness**       | Ensure that data collection, analysis, and use are free from bias or discrimination. | Audit algorithms to prevent bias in hiring, lending, or customer segmentation. |
| **Transparency**   | Be open about how and why data is collected, processed, and shared.                  | Publish privacy notices and explainable AI documentation to build trust.       |
| **Accountability** | Assign responsibility for data use decisions and their consequences.                 | Governance councils review ethical data use and report findings.               |
| **Consent**        | Collect and use data with informed, voluntary agreement from individuals.            | Obtain new consent when reusing customer data for another purpose.             |
| **Privacy**        | Protect individuals' rights to control their personal data.                          | Mask personal identifiers and minimize access to sensitive data.               |
| **Stewardship**    | Manage data as a valuable asset with care and integrity.                             | Apply retention, quality, and security policies consistently.                  |
| **Lawfulness**     | Comply with all applicable data protection regulations and obligations.              | Follow GDPR, HIPAA, or local privacy laws in operations.                       |

- These principles align with the DAMA-DMBOK2 framework.
- Always choose actions that promote fairness, accountability, and respect for individuals and their data.
