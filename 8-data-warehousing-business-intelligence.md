# 1. What is Data Warehousing and Business Intelligence

- "Data warehousing is the secure electronic storage of information by a business or other organization. The goal of data warehousing is to create a collection of historical data that can be retrieved and analyzed to provide useful insight into the organization's operations."
- Data warehousing is a vital component of business intelligence. That wider term encompasses the information infrastructure that modern businesses use to track their past successes and failures and inform their decisions for the future.
  - _Source: Jake Frankenfield - Investopedia_

# 3. What is a Data Warehouse (DW/DWH)

- Data Warehouse (DW or DWH), and sometimes also referred to as Enterprise
- Data Warehouse (EDW) is a central repository of integrated data from one or more different sources.
- Data Warehouses are used to store current and historical data in one single place.

# 10. Business Intelligence vs Business Analytics

- _"The primary distinction between business intelligence and business analytics is their focus on when events occur.**Business intelligence** focuses on current and past events captured in the data, while **business analytics** focuses on what is most likely to happen in the future."_
  - _[Source](www.mixpanel.com)_
