# 3. Causes of Poor Data Quality

- _Where Data Problems Begin._
- Human error during data entry or updates.
- **Integration issues:** Inconsistent formats or mismatched schemas.
- No clear standards or validation rules in systems.
- Incomplete or missing data from key sources.
- **Data decay:** Information becomes outdated over time.
- No data ownership or weak governance practices.
- Quality issues often start small - but grow quickly if ignored.

# 4. Assessing & Measuring Data Quality

- _How to Evaluate and Track Data Quality._
- **Data profiling:** Automatically scan data to find issues.
- **Metrics & KPIs:** Measure completeness, accuracy, and consistency.
- **Quality scorecards:** Visualize performance across systems.
- **Sampling & audits:** Spot-check and verify data quality.
- **Thresholds & tolerances:** Define acceptable data quality levels.
- Measurement creates the baseline for continuous improvement.

# 5. Data Cleansing & Standardization

- _Transforming Messy Data into Clean, Usable Information._
- **Cleansing:** Correct errors, fill gaps, and fix invalid values.
- **Deduplication:** Remove repeated or overlapping records.
- **Standardization:** Use consistent formats, names, and codes.
- **Enrichment:** Add missing info from trusted external sources.
- **Automation:** Apply validation rules to prevent future issues.
- **Tools:** Informatica, Talend, OpenRefine, Great Expectations.
- **Clean data** = trustworthy insights and confident decisions.

# 6. Data Quality Management Processes

- _Building Continuous Quality Improvement._
- Data quality is ongoing, not a one-time fix.
- **The continuous cycle**
  - Measure.
  - Improve.
  - Monitor.
  - Govern.
- **Key roles**
  - Data Stewards.
  - Data Owners.
  - Data Quality Analysts.
- Embed checks into data pipelines and ETL processes.
- Continuous monitoring builds a culture of data excellence.

# 8. Data Quality Governance & Policies

- _Setting Rules That Keep Data Reliable and Accountable._
- Governance defines **standards, rules, and responsibilities**.
- Create clear **policies** - who owns, validates, and maintains data.
- Align policies with **business goals and compliance requirements**.
- Use audits and dashboards to track adherence.
- Communication ensures **organization-wide consistency**.

# 9. Building a Data Quality Culture

- _Making Data Quality Part of Every Role._
- Quality begins with people, not just technology.
- Promote awareness and accountability across all teams.
- Provide training for accurate data entry and handling.
- Recognize and reward teams maintaining high data standards.
- Embed data quality metrics into KPIs and performance reviews.
- A quality culture ensures long-term, sustainable results.

# 10. Trends & Future of Data Quality

- _The Next Generation of Data Quality Management_
- **AI & Machine Learning:** Detect and fix data errors automatically.
- **Automation:** Real-time, continuous data validation.
- **Streaming & real-time quality:** Monitor as data flows.
- **Integration with DataOps & MLOps:** Quality built into pipelines.
- **Predictive quality:** Identify risks before they happen.
- **The future:** Proactive, intelligent, and fully automated quality control.
